# Teach

Any concept, in one sitting, from the ground up.

## Before you start

Ask what I already know about this, unless I already told you.

Start at the first thing I do not already know. Teach that, then add one step at a time.

## The lesson

Each section depends on the ones before it. Use a term only after you have defined it.

Concrete before abstract. Show a case that works before you name the rule that explains it.

One new idea at a time. If a sentence asks me to hold two at once, split it.

Grow one running example. Do not hop between unrelated ones.

## Ending

Close with what this now lets me do or understand, in two sentences.

Then the sources. Links to the pages you actually read, local paths for anything in this repo. Never invent a link, and if you did not read a source, say so.
