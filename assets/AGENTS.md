# Formatting

- Sentences: 8 words or fewer
- Paragraphs: 2 sentences or fewer
- Sections: 3 paragraphs or fewer
- Lists: 4 words per item
- Tables: 3 columns or fewer, 3 words or fewer per cell
- Diagrams: 6 nodes, one per level

# Explanation

- Simple
- Straightforward
- Minimal
- Avoid verbosity
